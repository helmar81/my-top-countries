## Day 1 -- Arrive in Casablanca

-   Visit Hassan II Mosque
-   Walk the Corniche
-   United Nations Square
-   Overnight: Casablanca

## Day 2 -- Rabat & Chefchaouen

-   Kasbah of the Udayas
-   Hassan Tower
-   Mausoleum of Mohammed V
-   Evening in Chefchaouen
-   Overnight: Chefchaouen

## Day 3 -- Chefchaouen → Fes

-   Explore the Blue City
-   Drive to Fes
-   Overnight: Fes

## Day 4 -- Discover Fes

-   Fes el Bali
-   Chouara Tannery
-   University of al-Qarawiyyin
-   Overnight: Fes

## Day 5 -- Fes → Merzouga

-   Middle Atlas Mountains
-   Camel trek
-   Desert camp
-   Stargazing

## Day 6 -- Merzouga → Aït Ben Haddou → Marrakech

-   Aït Benhaddou
-   Tizi n'Tichka Pass
-   Overnight: Marrakech

## Day 7 -- Marrakech

-   Jemaa el-Fnaa
-   Majorelle Garden
-   Bahia Palace

## Travel Tips

-   Best time: March--May or September--November
-   Currency: Moroccan Dirham (MAD)
-   Rent a car or join a guided tour
-   Dress modestly
-   Carry cash for small purchases
